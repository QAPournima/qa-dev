How I start

Context first. Changes later.

I wouldn’t change how a team works in my first week — even if I could already see things I wanted to fix. Experience and ideas travel with you. Context doesn’t.

First week

Observe

  • Meet the team and watch how they actually work.
  • Learn the product, the codebase, and how work ships.
  • See how people collaborate across roles.
  • Listen before suggesting changes.

First month

Investigate

  • Look at quality, delivery, environments, documentation, tooling and workload.
  • Find patterns instead of isolated problems.

First 90 days

Improve

  • Prioritize the few changes that actually matter.
  • Clarify ownership and expectations, then fix bottlenecks.
  • Introduce useful metrics.
  • Build a roadmap with the team, not for the team.
